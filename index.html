You can upload your raw clips, logos, or reference videos to a Google Drive folder and share the link with me directly on WhatsApp.

Short-form videos (Reels/Shorts) and thumbnails are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Long-form YouTube videos take 2 to 4 days depending on length.

How many revisions are included? +

Every project includes 2 free minor revisions (text changes, music tweaks, or pacing adjustments) so you are 100% happy with the final video.

For new clients, 50% advance is required to start the edit and the remaining 50% upon final approval before sending the watermark-free 4K file. UPI and bank transfers are accepted.
